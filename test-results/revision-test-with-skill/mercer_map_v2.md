**Distinguishing evidence (revised for H1).**
- *On the merits* it is still G19/G10 (what happened 10/31-11/3; the bill and refund; badge and video).
- *On the forum* the new distinguishing evidence is **G2 (the certified-letter receipt date) and the hospital's post-2/24 correspondence (G20).**
  - Receipt on or after 1/26, or any acknowledgment: our timeliness argument stands, and the waiver theory reduces to a recipient and manner quibble. That favors restoring Path A as leverage.
  - Receipt 1/22-1/24 and a formal rejection: the waiver theory is strong, Path A is likely lost, and C (Article 78, §2801-b) becomes primary.
  - Steps: USPS tracking from the article number (hospital copy, or Mercer's notice slip or envelope); the return-receipt card (hospital, through a demand); prior counsel's file. Each takes 1-3 days.
