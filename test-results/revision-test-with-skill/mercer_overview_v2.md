1. Dr. Evan Mercer, an OB/GYN employed by Glenhaven Medical Practice with privileges at county-owned NCGH, was put on probation on 12/11/2025 ("We want you to be successful", R1/03 Plan p.3).

4. The hospital reported him to the NPDB (1/23) and OPMC (2/3) (R1/05 NPDB; R2/OPMC).
5. Disputed: whether the peer review substantially supports "care below the standard." The only outside reviewer found the standard of care met on both charts it reviewed (R2/Summary p.5). Also disputed: whether the process was fair, and whether the falsified H&P, which Mercer admitted on the record, was a documentation lapse already disciplined or evidence of fraud (R2/Notes pp.1-2). **Under H1, the threshold question becomes whether Mercer still has a hearing at all.**
