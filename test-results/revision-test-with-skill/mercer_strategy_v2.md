## Recommendation: **UNCERTAIN, with a provisional modification**

**Holds:** B is the goal, C protects it, D is held. The merits theory is unchanged.

**Changes:** A drops from "live track" to "something to win back." The first step changes from demanding that a hearing be scheduled to **(1) proving the certified-receipt date and the hospital's response, and (2) sending a confirming request to the right officer by the right method.** C's Article 78 may become the primary forum.

That is consistent with v1's own Uncertainty table, which predicted this shift if A1 failed. v1 named the right premise. It underweighted the recipient and manner defects and the cascade into P3(v) and Path E.

**The single step that resolves it:** G2 plus G20, which is the certified-receipt date and the hospital's post-2/24 correspondence.
- If receipt was on or after Mon 1/26, **or** the hospital acknowledged the email: revert largely to v1 (A as the live track, with a threshold argument).

B stays the goal. Refer the prior-counsel question to separate counsel.
