5. Skill design

Two skills

case-strategy-tutor has seven stages, from sources to revision. Each ends with a question you must answer. It gives you three files to bring back next time.

citation-check enforces one rule. Every fact gets a page cite, a legal cite, or a label.

Each rule traces to Mercer. Typed labels led to the scan check. The NPDB miss led to the premise check.
