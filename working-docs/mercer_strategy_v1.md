## Recommendation
**Lead with A as the live track and B as the goal, with C filed to protect both. Hold D until the client interview.**

Why it beats the strongest alternative (an immediate negotiated exit, B alone): today the hospital's best offer is the lapsed 2/2 draft, which waives the hearing and says nothing about the NPDB (R1/05 Draft Separation ¶6). Negotiating without a live hearing and preserved claims gives Mercer nothing to trade. The overdue hearing, the 14-day lapse, ICRO and the §1983 exposure are worth something only while they can still be used.

Why not A alone: the hearing's structure favors the hospital (burden, deferential standard, no attorney examination, the Board reviewing itself). Mercer's own testimony could feed OPMC. A loss is worse for the ultimate objective than a negotiated revision.
