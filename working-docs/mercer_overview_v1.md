## The dispute in five sentences
1. Dr. Evan Mercer, an OB/GYN employed by Glenhaven Medical Practice with privileges at county-owned NCGH, was put on probation on 12/11/2025 ("We want you to be successful", R1/03 Plan p.3).
2. The next day he got a without-cause 90-day employment notice, and the CEO and CMO commissioned a chart review by the department chair, Dr. Venn (R1/03 90-Day Notice; R2/Venn p.1).
3. He was summarily suspended as an imminent danger on 12/16 or 12/19, with no patient named. The Board terminated all privileges on 1/21/2026, a day after the MEC recommended only sustaining the suspension (R1/03 12-16 MEC Letter; R2/MEC Letter p.1; R2/Board Minutes p.1).
4. The hospital reported him to the NPDB (1/23) and OPMC (2/3) (R1/05 NPDB; R2/OPMC).
5. Disputed: whether the peer review substantially supports "care below the standard." The only outside reviewer rated Case A "Standard of care met" and Case B "Standard of care met with some concerns" (R2/Summary p.5). Also disputed: whether the process was fair, and whether Mercer's falsified H&P, which he admitted on the record, is a documentation lapse already disciplined or evidence of fraud (R2/Notes pp.1-2).
